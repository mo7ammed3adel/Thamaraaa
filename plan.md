Head Account Manager:

The Head Account Manager is the first link that receives the client after the deal is closed by the sales team. The dashboard displays a list of all clients transferred to them from the Sales Manager, and each client in that list shows their name, entry date, contact information, and the package or service they agreed upon. When clicking on any client, a detailed page opens showing the client's complete journey from the moment they first entered with the TeleSales team, including the notes written by the TeleSales agent about the client, the notes written by the Sales agent, and all remarks recorded on the client at any stage of their journey before reaching the Head Account Manager.
From that same page, the Head Account Manager has the authority to assign the client to an Account Manager Agent on their team, or assign directly to the Head Technical if the matter requires technical team involvement, or assign to the Head SEO if the client primarily needs SEO services. This assignment is done through an Assign button on the client's page that opens a list of available people in each department to choose from.
The Head Account Manager also has the authority to write a Warning on any client in their dashboard, whether they are the one identifying a problem or receiving a complaint. Clicking the Warning button opens a window where they write the details of the problem or complaint, and once sent, the Warning is pushed as a mandatory popup to every employee who has that client in their dashboard. Each of those employees cannot interact with anything in the system until they click the "Mark as Read" button. The Head Account Manager themselves also receives Warning popups sent by an Account Manager Agent or by Sales if someone else writes a Warning on a client in their dashboard.
The dashboard also allows them to see, for each client, which employees are currently working on them, a list of all tasks assigned to that client across all departments, the status of each task whether hold, in progress, or done, and the date each task was created and completed.

Account Manager Agent:

The Account Manager Agent is the person directly responsible for the client from the moment the Head assigns the client to them. The dashboard displays a list of all clients assigned to them, and clicking on any client opens a comprehensive page showing the client's complete journey from the very beginning, meaning from the moment the client first entered with TeleSales all the way to the current moment. On this page they find the notes written by the TeleSales agent during the discovery and qualification stage, the notes written by the Sales agent during the persuasion, negotiation, and deal closing stage, and all remarks recorded on the client by any employee in the company at any stage. The purpose of this page is for them to begin their work with a full understanding of the client without needing to call anyone or ask questions.
The Account Manager Agent has the authority to write new notes on the client that get saved to their file and become visible to everyone who views that client's file. They also have the authority to write a Warning on the client using the same mechanism described above, whether the client is complaining or there is a problem with delivery or work quality.
The dashboard allows them to see, for each of their clients, who the complete team working on them is, meaning who the Media Buyer is, who the Social Media agent is, who the SEO agent is, and all team members assigned to that client at any stage. They can also see a complete list of all tasks created for the client across all departments, with the status of each task, who created it and when, and the start and completion dates if the task is finished.
The Account Manager Agent also receives Warning popups sent by anyone else who writes a Warning on a client in their dashboard, and they are required to confirm they have read it before continuing their work.

Head Technical:

The Head Technical is responsible for the entire technical team which includes Social Media and Media Buyer. They receive clients from the Head Account Manager only, not from anyone else. The dashboard displays a list of clients transferred to them, and clicking on any client opens a page with the client's complete data and journey, and the notes written by every employee on that client from TeleSales all the way to the Account Manager Agent. They read this information to understand the client's needs and the agreement made with them before beginning to distribute the work.
From the client's page, the Head Technical has the authority to assign the client to the Team Leader Social Media, or to the Team Leader Media Buyer, or to both simultaneously if the agreement covers both services. There is no authority to assign to the Head SEO as that is a separate track not under their management.
They can view all tasks created for their clients and the status of each task, and they receive Warning popups for clients in their dashboard and are required to confirm they have read them.

Head SEO:

The Head SEO receives clients directly from the Head Account Manager or from the Account Manager Agent, and this is a completely independent track from the Head Technical. The dashboard displays the clients transferred to them along with their complete data and all team notes on them. Clicking on a client shows their journey and all details from the beginning so they can understand the client's SEO-specific needs.
They have the authority to assign the client to the Team Leader SEO under their management. They monitor the status of SEO-related tasks for each of their clients, and they receive Warning popups and are required to confirm they have read them.

Team Leader SEO:

The Team Leader SEO receives clients from the Head SEO only. The dashboard displays the clients transferred to them, and they can open each client's page to see their data and the notes written by all employees on that client from the beginning of their journey to the present. The purpose is for them to have a solid understanding of the client before assigning tasks.
They have the authority to create tasks from their dashboard and assign them to the SEO Agent or Content SEO Agent under their management. The task specifies the type of work required and its details. They monitor the status and timeline of every task, and they receive Warning popups and are required to confirm they have read them.

Agent SEO:

The SEO Agent receives tasks from the Team Leader SEO. The dashboard displays the tasks assigned to them with all details of each task, and they can open the client's page associated with each task to see the notes of all employees from the beginning of the client's journey. The purpose is for them to properly understand the client and work in a way that aligns with the client's actual requirements that were discussed with TeleSales, Sales, and the Account Manager.
They have the authority to update the status of their tasks from hold to in progress to done. They also have the authority to create design tasks from their dashboard and assign them to the Team Leader Graphic Designer or to the Team Leader UI/UX, and within that task they upload the task link and specify the type of work required. The task goes to the Team Leader first who then distributes it to their Agent. They receive Warning popups and are required to confirm they have read them.

Agent Content SEO:

The mechanism is identical to the SEO Agent. They receive tasks from the Team Leader SEO and can open the client's file to see all team notes on them. They update the status of their tasks. They have the authority to create design tasks assigned to the Team Leader Graphic Designer or Team Leader UI/UX if they need designs for their content. They receive Warning popups and are required to confirm they have read them.

Team Leader Social Media:

The Team Leader Social Media receives clients from the Head Technical only. The dashboard displays the clients transferred to them, and they can open each client's page to see the notes of all employees on them from the beginning of their journey. They read this information to understand the client and their social media needs before distributing.
They have the authority to assign the client to one or more Social Media Agents on their team. They monitor the work and tasks on the clients, and they receive Warning popups and are required to confirm they have read them.

Agent Social Media:

The Social Media Agent receives clients from the Team Leader Social Media. The dashboard displays the clients assigned to them, and they can open each client's page to see the notes of all employees from the moment the client first entered the company. The purpose is for them to properly understand the client and their requirements so they can create content that aligns with the client's actual personality and needs.
They have the authority to create design tasks from their dashboard. If they need a static design they create a task assigned to the Team Leader Graphic Designer, if they need motion or video editing they create a task assigned to the Team Leader Motion Graphic, and if they need interface design they create a task assigned to the Team Leader UI/UX. In every task they upload the task link and specify the type of work required. The task goes to the relevant Team Leader first who then distributes it to their Agent. They update the status of their tasks, and they receive Warning popups and are required to confirm they have read them.

Team Leader Media Buyer:

The Team Leader Media Buyer receives clients from the Head Technical only. The dashboard displays the clients transferred to them, and they can open each client's page to see all team notes on them from the beginning. They read the details to understand the client agreement, budgets, and advertising objectives.
They have the authority to assign the client to a Media Buyer Agent on their team. They monitor the work and tasks, and they receive Warning popups and are required to confirm they have read them.

Agent Media Buyer:

The Media Buyer Agent receives clients from the Team Leader Media Buyer. The dashboard displays the clients assigned to them, and they can open each client's page to see the notes of all employees from the beginning of the client's journey. The purpose is for them to understand the client, their needs, budgets, and advertising goals before launching campaigns.
They have the authority to create design tasks from their dashboard. If they need a static creative they create a task assigned to the Team Leader Graphic Designer, if they need video or motion they create a task assigned to the Team Leader Motion Graphic, and if they need interface design they create a task assigned to the Team Leader UI/UX. They upload the task link and specify its type, and the task goes to the relevant Team Leader first. They update the status of their tasks, and they receive Warning popups and are required to confirm they have read them.

Leader Graphic Designer:

The Leader Graphic Designer receives static design tasks coming from the Social Media Agent, Media Buyer Agent, SEO Agent, or Content SEO Agent. The dashboard displays a list of all incoming tasks with every detail of each task including its link, the type of work required, and the date it was created. They can open the client's page associated with each task to see the full team notes, so that both they and the assigned Agent properly understand the client.
They have the authority to assign that task to a Graphic Designer Agent on their team. They monitor the status and timeline of every task, and they receive Warning popups and are required to confirm they have read them.

Agent Graphic Designer:

The Graphic Designer Agent receives tasks from the Leader Graphic Designer only. The dashboard displays the tasks assigned to them with the details and links of each task. They can open the client's page associated with each task to see the notes of all employees from the beginning of the client's journey to the present. This helps them understand what the client likes and dislikes and what their actual requirements are, even if those details were not fully captured in the task brief.
They have the authority to update the status of their task from hold to in progress to done. They receive Warning popups and are required to confirm they have read them before continuing in the system.

Leader Motion Graphic:

The Leader Motion Graphic receives motion and video editing tasks coming from the Social Media Agent or the Media Buyer Agent. The dashboard displays a list of incoming tasks with all their details. They can open the client's page to see the full team notes. They have the authority to assign the task to a Motion Graphic Agent on their team. They monitor the status and timeline of tasks, and they receive Warning popups and are required to confirm they have read them.

Agent Motion Graphic:

They can open the client's page to see all team notes from the beginning of the client's journey so they can understand the client and their requirements and work in a way that matches them. They have the authority to update the task status from hold to in progress to done. They receive Warning popups and are required to confirm they have read them.

Leader UI/UX:

The Leader UI/UX receives tasks coming from the SEO Agent, Content SEO Agent, Social Media Agent, or Media Buyer Agent. The dashboard displays the incoming tasks with their details. They can open the client's page to see the full team notes. They have the authority to assign the task to a UI/UX Agent on their team. They monitor the status and timeline of tasks, and they receive Warning popups and are required to confirm they have read them.

Agent UI/UX:

Warning System — Unified Details for All Roles:

The Warning is written by the Account Manager Agent or the Sales agent when a client complains or there is a problem with the work. They click the Warning button on the client's page, write the details of the problem, and send it. At that moment a mandatory popup is pushed to every employee in the company who has that client in their dashboard, meaning everyone with any connection to that client receives this popup on their screen. The popup blocks them from using anything in the system until they click the "Mark as Read" button, after which their confirmation is logged and the popup is dismissed so they can continue their work.

Chief Sales, HR Manager, and Accountant:

These three administrative roles have no involvement in the client journey or in the distribution and task workflow. The Chief Sales dashboard is administrative and displays sales team performance reports and deal statistics. The HR Manager dashboard covers employee data, role management, and account administration. The Accountant dashboard covers financial data, contracts, invoices, and payments. These roles are completely isolated from the Warning system and from the client journey.
